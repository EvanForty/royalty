# Royalty

Every holder earns.

Royalty is the revenue-sharing launchpad of Solana. Every coin launched on Royalty pays its holders a share of its creator fees in SOL every 10 minutes. No staking and no locking: hold the token and your share follows your balance, measured with time-weighted snapshots.

- 70% of creator fees to the coin's holders
- 20% to $ROYALTY holders
- 10% to the treasury

Creators can also pick a Builder (60% holders, 10% creator) or Founder (50% holders, 20% creator) setup at launch.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole site (HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file)
- `logo.png`: the Royalty logo, also used as the favicon

## Deploy with GitHub Pages

1. Push these files to the root of your repository.
2. Go to Settings → Pages, choose Deploy from a branch, pick `main` and `/ (root)`, then save.

It also works on Vercel, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages with no build step.
