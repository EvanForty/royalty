Four rules every coin on Royalty follows.

Hold to earn

Holders get SOL in proportion to how much of the eligible supply they own.

No staking

Your tokens stay in your wallet, liquid and transferable.

Automatic

Rewards are calculated and paid on a fixed schedule, every 10 minutes.

Revenue-backed

Every payout comes from real creator fees, never newly minted tokens.

How Royalty works

Five steps. After launch, everything runs on its own.

1

Launch a token

Every coin gets its own royalty pool the moment it launches.

2

Trading makes fees

Creator fees from every trade are routed to the pool.

3

Snapshot holders

Balances are measured over time, so last-second buys don’t game the payout.

4

Pay out SOL

The pool is split across holders by their share of the eligible supply.

5

Claim or reinvest

Claim your SOL anytime, or use it to buy more of the coin.
